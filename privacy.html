TAMTAGYM Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

TAMTAGYM is a gym management application designed to help gym owners manage member information, memberships, payments and renewals.

1. Information the App Handles

Depending on how you use the app, it may store gym member details, contact numbers, membership dates, payment amounts, payment history and related gym records.

2. Local Storage

Gym records may be stored locally on your device. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and its stored information.

3. Google Account and Google Drive

The app uses Google sign-in when you choose to sign in with Google. If you separately authorize Google Drive access, the app may use that access to create or retrieve backup files for your gym records.

Google Drive backup and restore features are available only when implemented and enabled in the app. You can choose whether to authorize Drive access. Google services are subject to Google's own privacy policy and terms.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide gym management features, maintain membership and payment records, and support backup or restore operations that you choose to perform.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google services when you use sign-in or authorize Drive features. You should only enter member information that you are authorized to collect and manage.

6. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable care should be taken to protect stored records and backup files. Local records and Drive backups may remain until you delete them or remove them using the relevant available controls. No method of storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

TAMTAGYM is intended for gym management by gym owners and operators, not for children to create their own accounts.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer using the developer contact email provided on the app's official Google authorization or store listing.
